# Phased Goal Coordination And Workstream Delivery

Read this branch for selected Phased or Team work.
The shared [Skill protections](../SKILL.md#shared-protections-and-method-autonomy)
and [coordination](coordination-and-recovery.md) own the common contract.

## Arrangement-v2 Responsibilities

| Responsibility | Owns |
|---|---|
| O | Goals, priorities, cross-workstream tradeoffs, total-cost decisions and agreed outcome acceptance |
| Direct D | Authorized exploration, planning, implementation, checks, repair and delivery |
| Selected Team P | Workstream contract, coordination, finding disposition and stable acceptance |
| Selected Team E | Implementation, checks, repair and delivery within that contract |
| R | Read-only independent judgment on the action premise, product and necessary consumers |

Each workstream defaults to Direct. Select Team for a concrete need for separate
contract/acceptance responsibility. Technical owners coordinate authorized
interfaces directly; O receives significant direction/cost choices before further
investment and accepts agreed results. D/E choose ordinary methods and recovery
actions under [Failures And Recovery](coordination-and-recovery.md#failures-and-recovery).

The original author repairs, the same valid R reviews affected changes, and the
designated owner accepts at actual outcome checkpoints. Follow
[Authorized Progression](coordination-and-recovery.md#authorized-progression) and
[Review Input](coordination-and-recovery.md#review-input-at-any-level).
Those cover recovery rationale and implementation as separate conclusions.
O's coordination does not add a method-approval or duplicate technical-review step.
